\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The 4th MAVIC-T challenge~\cite{lowMultimodalAerialView2024,bowald3rdMultimodalAerial2025} defines four cross-modal aerial image translation tasks: SAR$\rightarrow$EO, SAR$\rightarrow$RGB, SAR$\rightarrow$IR, and RGB$\rightarrow$IR. We propose \textbf{EarthBridge}, which applies DBIM~\cite{zhengDiffusionBridgeImplicit2025} to three tasks and CUT~\cite{parkContrastiveLearningUnpaired2020} to SAR$\rightarrow$IR, where the large modality gap favors the contrastive approach. The remainder of this report focuses on our method, implementation details, and key findings.
