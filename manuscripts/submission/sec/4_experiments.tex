\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Dataset and Preprocessing}
\label{sec:dataset}

We use the MAVIC-T 2025 dataset from the challenge organizers, with imagery from UNICORN, USGS EROS HRO, and UMBRA across four regions: Bingham, Centerfield, Manhattan, and UC Davis. \cref{tab:dataset_tasks} summarizes the four translation tasks. SAR$\rightarrow$EO operates at 256$\times$256; the other three tasks use 1024$\times$1024. For SAR$\rightarrow$IR and SAR$\rightarrow$RGB, we spatially align SAR scenes with optical mosaics before resampling. We also create crop-augmented training sets for the 1024$\times$1024 tasks to increase data diversity. Validation and test sets contain 60 samples per task (3,586 for SAR$\rightarrow$EO test).

\subsection{Implementation Details}
\label{sec:impl_details}

We follow the Diffusion Bridge Foundation architecture with task-specific scaling (small config for SAR$\rightarrow$EO at 256px; medium config for 1024px tasks). All models use the Prodigy optimizer, bfloat16 mixed precision, and DBIM deterministic sampling. Full architectural details, hyperparameters, and per-task configurations are provided in the appendix (\cref{sec:appendix}).

\subsection{Failure Case and Challenging Scenario Analysis}
\label{sec:failure_cases}

To provide a balanced assessment, we analyze cases where EarthBridge degrades or fails. We select representative challenging samples from the validation set by ranking on worst LPIPS and L1 scores, then inspect them visually. \cref{fig:failure_cases} shows three typical failure modes.

    \centering
    \begin{subfigure}[t]{0.32\textwidth}
        \centering
        \fbox{\rule{0pt}{1.5in} \rule{\linewidth}{0pt}}

        \label{fig:failure_speckle}
    \end{subfigure}
    \hfill
    \begin{subfigure}[t]{0.32\textwidth}
        \centering
        \fbox{\rule{0pt}{1.5in} \rule{\linewidth}{0pt}}

        \label{fig:failure_urban}
    \end{subfigure}
    \hfill
    \begin{subfigure}[t]{0.32\textwidth}
        \centering
        \fbox{\rule{0pt}{1.5in} \rule{\linewidth}{0pt}}
        \caption{Illumination mismatch: Strong shadows or extreme lighting in RGB$\rightarrow$IR may lead to inconsistent thermal appearance.}
        \label{fig:failure_illumination}
    \end{subfigure}
    \caption{Failure cases and challenging scenarios. Each column shows source, EarthBridge output, and ground truth for samples selected by worst LPIPS/L1 on the validation set. (a) Speckle ambiguity in SAR inputs. (b) Complex urban layouts. (c) Illumination mismatch in RGB$\rightarrow$IR.}
    \label{fig:failure_cases}
\end{figure*}

These failure modes highlight inherent limitations of cross-modal translation: SAR speckle does not uniquely determine optical texture, dense urban geometry increases structural ambiguity, and RGB-to-IR mapping is underconstrained under varying illumination. Acknowledging these limitations adds credibility and guides future work toward more robust handling of edge cases.

\subsection{Ablation Study}
\label{sec:ablation}

We conduct ablation studies on sampling efficiency and training strategies.

\paragraph{Effect of Sampling Steps.}
We evaluate the impact of the number of sampling steps (NFE) on the translation quality using the SAR$\rightarrow$EO task (\cref{fig:ablation_qualitative}). Due to the deterministic DBIM sampling formulation, our model maintains high structural and perceptual fidelity even at low NFE. We observe that 40 steps provide an optimal balance between inference speed and generation quality.
