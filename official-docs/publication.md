# Publication Oportunities

All participants are warmly invited to prepare and submit a **full research paper** describing their methods, findings, and contributions developed within the scope of this challenge. Accepted papers will be published in the **official proceedings of the CVPR 2026 workshop**.

Manuscripts must follow the **[CVPR 2026 Author Guidelines](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuidelines)**. All submissions will undergo a **double-blind peer review process.** More information at [PBVS website](https://pbvs-workshop.github.io/).
