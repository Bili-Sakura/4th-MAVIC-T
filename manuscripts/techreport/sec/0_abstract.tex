\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{EarthBridge}, our solution for the 4th MAVIC-T challenge. We apply Diffusion Bridge Implicit Models (DBIM)~\cite{zhengDiffusionBridgeImplicit2025} to three translation tasks (SAR$\rightarrow$EO, SAR$\rightarrow$RGB, RGB$\rightarrow$IR) and Contrastive Unpaired Translation (CUT) for SAR$\rightarrow$IR. Our solution achieves the second position overall. We report composite scores of 0.269, 0.576, 0.464, and 0.200 on SAR$\rightarrow$EO, SAR$\rightarrow$RGB, SAR$\rightarrow$IR, and RGB$\rightarrow$IR respectively, with an average of 0.38.
\end{abstract}
