\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

Image-to-image translation has been extensively studied in computer vision, with foundational works including CycleGAN for unpaired translation \citep{zhuUnpairedImageToImageTranslation2017} and Pix2PixHD for high-resolution conditional generation \citep{wangHighResolutionImageSynthesis2018}. Recent advances in remote sensing image-to-image translation have explored various approaches, including GAN-based methods for SAR-to-optical translation \citep{huGanbasedSAROptical2022, wangSARtoOpticalImageTranslation2022}, conditional diffusion models with spatial-frequency refinement \citep{qinConditionalDiffusionModel2024}, efficient end-to-end diffusion models for one-step translation \citep{qinEfficientEndtoEndDiffusion2025}, and adaptive domain shift techniques in diffusion models \citep{wangAdaptiveDomainShift2026}.
