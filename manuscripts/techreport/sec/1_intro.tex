\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The 4th MAVIC-T challenge~\cite{lowMultimodalAerialView2024,bowald3rdMultimodalAerial2025} defines four cross-modal aerial image translation tasks: SAR$\rightarrow$EO, SAR$\rightarrow$RGB, SAR$\rightarrow$IR, and RGB$\rightarrow$IR. Prior art ranges from paired/unpaired GAN baselines~\cite{isolaImageToImageTranslationConditional2017,zhuUnpairedImageToImageTranslation2017} and diffusion bridge models~\cite{zhouDenoisingDiffusionBridge2024,liuI2SBImagetoImageSchrodinger2023} to contrastive unpaired translation~\cite{parkContrastiveLearningUnpaired2020}. We propose \textbf{EarthBridge}, which applies DBIM~\cite{zhengDiffusionBridgeImplicit2025}---a non-Markovian diffusion bridge enabling deterministic sampling---to three tasks and CUT~\cite{parkContrastiveLearningUnpaired2020} to SAR$\rightarrow$IR where the large modality gap favors the contrastive approach. The remainder of this report focuses on our method, implementation details, and key findings.
