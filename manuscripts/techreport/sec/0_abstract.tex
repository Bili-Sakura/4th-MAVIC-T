\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{EarthBridge}, our solution for the 4th MAVIC-T challenge. We apply Diffusion Bridge Implicit Models (DBIM) to three translation tasks (SAR$\rightarrow$EO, SAR$\rightarrow$RGB, RGB$\rightarrow$IR) and Contrastive Unpaired Translation (CUT) for SAR$\rightarrow$IR. Our UNet denoiser is conditioned on the source image via channel concatenation and trained with Karras-weighted bridge scalings. Deterministic DBIM sampling with ``booting noise'' enables efficient high-fidelity translation across all four modality pairs.
\end{abstract}
