\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}



The Multi-modal Aerial View Image Challenge for cross domain image translation~\cite{lowMultimodalAerialView2024,lowMultimodalAerialView2023,bowald3rdMultimodalAerial2025} is a leading challenge held in conjunction with the Perception Beyond the Visible Spectrum (PBVS) workshop from 2023 to 2025. Data collected across multiple sensing modalities---such as Electro-Optical (EO), Infrared (IR), and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR), providing complementary perspectives on the same scene. However, data coverage limitations and modality-specific constraints often hinder the full utilization of these diverse sources. For instance, while SAR sensors offer all-weather capabilities and can penetrate atmospheric obstructions, their imagery is complex to interpret and publicly available datasets are scarce. Conversely, widely available EO imagery is limited by lighting conditions and atmospheric visibility. Domain translation seeks to bridge this gap by converting one modality into another, enabling cross-modal data synthesis and enhancing sensor interoperability. By focusing on translation tasks such as RGB$\rightarrow$IR, SAR$\rightarrow$EO, SAR$\rightarrow$IR, and SAR$\rightarrow$RGB, the MAVIC-T challenge aims to bolster existing data volume, increase data diversity across regions, and serve as a platform for advancing conditioned image synthesis techniques, ultimately fostering the development of more adaptable and robust models for multi-modal image analysis.

SAR images possess unique attributes that present challenges for both human observers and vision artificial intelligence (AI) models to interpret, owing to their electromagnetic (EM) characteristics~\cite{majumder2020deep,huangClassificationLargeScaleHighResolution2021,huangDeepSARNetLearning2020,huangHDECTFAUnsupervisedLearning2021,huangPhysicallyExplainableCNN2022}.

% paragraph: our solution
{\color{gray}
In this paper, we propose EarthBridge, a diffusion-based framework for cross-modal aerial image translation built on Denoising Diffusion Bridge Models (DDBM). Our approach conditions the bridge process on the source image via channel concatenation and trains a UNet denoiser under Karras-weighted bridge scalings. We further incorporate optional representation alignment with frozen MaRS encoders and a MAVIC-T-aware auxiliary loss to ensure that translated images preserve the geometric layout of the source modality while achieving high perceptual fidelity.

% paragraph: Contributions
{\color{gray}
Our main contributions are summarized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item We propose \textbf{EarthBridge}, a novel Denoising Diffusion Bridge Model (DDBM)-based framework for paired cross-modal aerial image translation, which conditions the bridge process directly on the source image and leverages Karras-weighted bridge scalings for robust training across EO, IR, and SAR modalities.
    \item We introduce a MAVIC-T-aware auxiliary loss tailored to the challenge objectives (LPIPS and L1), guiding the model to directly optimize evaluation metrics relevant to cross-modal translation tasks.
    \item We incorporate modality-specific representation alignment by integrating frozen MaRS encoders pre-trained on large-scale remote sensing data, enhancing semantic consistency between generated images and source modalities.
    \item For high-resolution translation scenarios, we employ latent-space modeling with a pre-trained VAE to reduce memory demands without sacrificing generative fidelity.
    \item We conduct comprehensive experiments on all four MAVIC-T tasks at multiple resolutions, demonstrating EarthBridge's superior performance against strong baseline methods and providing detailed comparisons in terms of both objective metrics and qualitative results.
\end{itemize}
